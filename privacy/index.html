Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

MichiganWorkFromHome.Com respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide through this website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information when you use our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you complete a form on this website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Answers to questions presented through the website
  • Additional information submitted through the optional "Others" field

Some answers may relate to a person's health, disability, medical needs, Medicaid status, or ability to perform daily activities. Please provide only information that is necessary and that you are comfortable submitting.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this website may be used to:

  • Review your submission and responses.
  • Determine which information or resources may be relevant to your situation.
  • Contact you regarding your submission.
  • Organize and process submitted information.
  • Improve the website and the user experience.

3. Automated Processing

Information submitted through this website may be processed using automated systems to organize submissions and determine which information should be associated with a particular category or workflow.

Automated processing is intended to assist with organizing submissions and does not itself constitute an official determination of eligibility for the Michigan Medicaid Home Help Program.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with or processed by service providers that help operate the website, process submissions, automate workflows, or store submitted information. Such providers may include hosting, automation, database, spreadsheet, email, or other technology services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of the website, its users, or others.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information submitted through this website against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no website, electronic transmission, or method of storing information can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should therefore avoid submitting information that is not necessary for your inquiry.

6. How Long We Keep Your Information

We may retain submitted information for as long as it is reasonably necessary to process submissions, provide requested assistance, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and maintain the security of our systems.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary for website functionality, security, analytics, or improving the user experience.

If cookies or analytics services are used, additional information about those technologies may be provided as the website develops.

8. Third-Party Websites

This website may contain links to websites or resources operated by third parties. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of any third-party website you visit.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding personal information you have submitted to us. These may include requesting access to, correction of, or deletion of your information.

To make a privacy-related request, please contact the website operator using the contact information provided by the operator.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, services, technology, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated accordingly.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the information you have submitted through this website, please contact the operator of MichiganWorkFromHome.Com.
